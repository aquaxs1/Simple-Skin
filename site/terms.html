The mod

Nothing reaches us. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting and no server of ours. The mod runs on your computer and talks only to Mojang and, with live sync, to the Minecraft server you are playing on.

What it stores stays on your computer: the library, history and settings under config/simple-skin (a skin taken from another player keeps that player's name), and PNG exports under simple-skin/exports in your Minecraft folder. Deleting those folders removes all of it.

The mod contacts four hosts, all operated by Mojang:

Host Why
api.minecraftservices.com Upload your skin and change your active cape. Uses your existing session token.
api.mojang.com Resolve a username to a profile ID when you search for a player.
sessionserver.mojang.com Read a profile's public skin data.
textures.minecraft.net Download skin and cape images. All skin URLs are pinned to this host.

Your session token is only ever sent to api.minecraftservices.com, and only to perform an action you asked for. It is never written to disk by the mod.

With the visibility setting Upload to profile (the default) or Upload + rejoin, the skin you equip is uploaded to your Minecraft profile, where Mojang shows it to everyone, like any skin you set yourself. An outfit's cape choice is set on your profile too. Mojang's own privacy rules apply to your account.

Live sync is on by default. On a server that also runs Simple Skin, the skin you equip is sent to that server, which passes it on to the other players' Simple Skin clients and keeps it in memory only while you are online. On a server without the mod nothing is sent. Turn it off in the skin settings with Live sync: off.

With the visibility setting Only me, equipping a skin sends nothing anywhere: no live sync, no upload and no cape change on your profile.
