3. Privacy

The mod collects nothing. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting and no third-party server. Nothing about you is sent to the authors.

Everything the mod stores stays in your own Minecraft folder, under config/simple-skin. Deleting that folder removes all of it.

The mod contacts exactly three hosts, all operated by Mojang:

HostWhy
api.minecraftservices.comUpload your skin and change your active cape. Uses your existing session token.
api.mojang.comResolve a username to a profile ID when you search for a player.
sessionserver.mojang.comRead a profile's public skin data.
textures.minecraft.netDownload skin and cape images. All skin URLs are pinned to this host.

Your session token is only ever sent to api.minecraftservices.com, and only to perform an action you asked for. It is never written to disk by the mod.

This website serves static files and sets no cookies. It loads a web font from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. The site itself stores nothing about you. Your hosting provider may keep standard server access logs.
