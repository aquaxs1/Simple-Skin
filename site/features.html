Capabilities

What the mod can do

Skin library

Three tabs: History, your saved wardrobe, and everyone currently on the server.

Equip without restarting

Equip from a card, the settings screen or a per-skin hotkey — applied in the same tick.

Live sync to other players

On a Simple Skin server the swap reaches every other client over a plugin channel, instantly.

Profile upload with verification

Anywhere else the PNG goes to your Mojang profile, and the mod waits until Mojang really serves it.

Steal from the server

Copy anyone on the tab list with one click — wear it now or save it.

Search any username

Look up any name through Mojang's public API. The player need not be online.

Import from your computer

Pick a PNG, or drop files into config/simple-skin/import and they appear on their own.

Built-in editor

Remove overlay layers, graft a head from another skin, or replace a colour. The original is kept.

Outfits with capes

Pair a skin with a cape you own — keep it, hide it, or pick a specific one.

Hotkeys, shuffle and rotation

Bind any key to any saved skin, with a clash warning. Shuffle and timed rotation have their own keys.

3D preview

A real player model you can drag to rotate, so you can check the back before wearing it.

Export and delete

Write any skin back out as a PNG, or delete it behind a two-step confirm.

Ready to try it?

One jar, a couple of dependencies, and a key to press.
